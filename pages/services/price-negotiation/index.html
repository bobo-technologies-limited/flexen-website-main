THE SERVICE

Turning Supplier Quotes into Better Procurement Decisions

Finding a supplier is only one part of international sourcing. The price, payment terms, minimum order quantities, specifications and commercial conditions can significantly affect the overall cost of your procurement.

More Than Comparing Prices

FLEXEN supports businesses by helping them assess supplier quotations and engage suppliers around pricing and commercial requirements.

The objective is to help you understand your options before committing to an order.

Built Around Your Requirements

Negotiation can take into account factors such as order quantity, product specifications, packaging, delivery requirements and other agreed commercial considerations.

This creates a more structured procurement process from supplier selection through to order approval.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Price Negotiation FAQs

Here are some common questions businesses have about supplier price negotiation.

View All FAQs
Can FLEXEN negotiate with suppliers on my behalf?

FLEXEN can support supplier communication and commercial discussions as part of the sourcing process, based on the requirements and instructions provided by the client.

Does a lower unit price always mean a better deal?

Not necessarily. Product specifications, quantity, packaging, payment requirements, shipping and other commercial conditions should also be considered.

Can negotiation be based on order quantity?

Order quantity can be one of the factors considered when reviewing supplier offers and discussing commercial terms.

What happens after the price is agreed?

Once the procurement details are approved, the order can proceed to the appropriate payment, shipping and logistics stages.
