Bringing Key Sourcing Activities Together

International sourcing involves more than finding a supplier. Businesses may need to identify suitable products, compare suppliers, negotiate terms, coordinate payments and arrange transportation.

FLEXEN brings these activities into a structured sourcing process designed to help businesses move from an initial requirement to procurement and delivery coordination.
