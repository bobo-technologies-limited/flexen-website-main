China vs Turkey Sourcing: Which Market Should You Choose?

Understanding the key factors to consider when sourcing products from China or Turkey for your business.

FLEXEN · Sourcing & Procurement

Choosing a sourcing market is an important decision for any business purchasing products internationally. China and Turkey both provide access to manufacturers, wholesalers and trading businesses, but the right market depends on the product, specifications, quantity, budget and overall procurement requirements.

China vs Turkey: What Should You Consider?

There is no single sourcing market that is suitable for every product or business. Instead, buyers should consider several factors before deciding where to source.

1. Product Category

China has a large manufacturing ecosystem covering electronics, machinery, automotive components, packaging, construction products, furniture and many other categories.

Turkey also offers sourcing opportunities across manufacturing, construction materials, furniture, textiles, machinery, automotive products and other commercial categories.

2. Supplier Availability

The number and type of suppliers available can vary significantly between industries. Buyers should identify suppliers based on their exact product specifications rather than simply choosing a country.

3. Pricing

Product pricing should be evaluated together with quantities, specifications, packaging, shipping, duties and other procurement costs.

A lower supplier quotation does not necessarily mean a lower overall landed cost.

4. Minimum Order Quantities

Minimum order quantities can vary from one supplier to another. Businesses should clarify the required quantity before making a sourcing decision.

5. Logistics

Transportation requirements should form part of the sourcing decision from the beginning. Shipping method, cargo characteristics, destination and delivery timelines can all affect the final procurement cost.

6. Supplier Communication

Effective communication is important when discussing specifications, quantities, packaging, quality requirements and commercial terms.

China or Turkey: How Should a Business Decide?

Rather than asking which country is universally better, businesses should ask which market is appropriate for the specific product they are trying to source.

A useful sourcing process starts with defining the requirement, identifying suitable supplier options and comparing the commercial and logistical implications.
