1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how FLEXEN ("FLEXEN", "we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. They can help websites remember information about your visit, support functionality, improve performance, and provide information about how the website is used.

This policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service .

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files placed on your computer, mobile device, tablet, or other device when you visit a website.

Depending on how they are configured, cookies may allow a website to recognise your browser, remember preferences, maintain functionality, understand website usage, or improve your browsing experience.

First-Party Cookies

First-party cookies are cookies placed by the website you are visiting. In this case, they may be placed by FLEXEN or by technologies operating on behalf of FLEXEN.

Third-Party Cookies

Third-party cookies may be placed by external services or technologies used on a website. Their use depends on the tools and services implemented on the FLEXEN website.

3. Why We Use Cookies

FLEXEN may use cookies and similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Enabling essential website functionality.
  • Remembering certain preferences or settings.
  • Improving website navigation and usability.
  • Understanding how visitors interact with our website.
  • Monitoring website performance and reliability.
  • Supporting security and preventing misuse.
  • Improving our website, services, and content.

4. Types of Cookies We May Use

The cookies used on a website can generally be grouped into several categories.

Type Purpose Typical Use
Essential Required for core website functionality and security. Navigation, security, sessions and basic functionality.
Functional Helps remember preferences and settings. User preferences and interface settings.
Analytics Helps us understand website usage and performance. Traffic patterns, page interactions and performance measurement.
Marketing May be used to support relevant communications or advertising where implemented. Campaign measurement and advertising functionality.

Important: The specific cookies actually placed on your device depend on the features, technologies, and services active on the FLEXEN website at the time of your visit.

5. Essential Cookies

Essential cookies may be necessary for certain website functions to operate correctly.

These cookies may support functions such as navigation, security, session management, form functionality, consent preferences, and other core website operations.

Because these cookies may be necessary for the operation of the website, disabling them may affect certain functionality.

6. Functional Cookies

Functional cookies may allow the website to remember choices you make and provide enhanced functionality.

For example, these technologies may remember certain interface preferences or settings so that you do not have to configure them again during every visit.

7. Analytics and Performance Technologies

Where analytics technologies are implemented, FLEXEN may use them to understand how visitors interact with the website.

This information may help us understand which pages are visited, how visitors navigate the website, whether certain features are being used, and where improvements may be needed.

Any analytics technology used on the website should be configured and managed in accordance with applicable privacy requirements and our Privacy Policy.

8. Third-Party Technologies

Certain website features may rely on services provided by third parties.

Where third-party technologies place cookies or collect information through the website, their processing may also be governed by the relevant third party's privacy and cookie policies.

FLEXEN does not represent that every third-party service will use cookies in the same manner. Their specific practices depend on the services implemented and their respective configurations.

9. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Depending on your browser and the technologies implemented on the website, you may be able to control or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Most modern browsers allow users to:

  • View stored cookies.
  • Delete existing cookies.
  • Block cookies from specific websites.
  • Block third-party cookies.
  • Configure browser notifications when cookies are being stored.

Please note: Blocking or deleting cookies may affect the functionality, security, or performance of certain parts of the website.

10. Browser Cookie Controls

Cookie controls are generally available through the privacy, security, or settings section of your web browser.

Because browsers differ, the exact steps for managing cookies may vary depending on whether you use Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, Safari, Opera, or another browser.

You should consult the documentation provided by your browser provider for the most accurate instructions applicable to your device and browser version.

11. Cookie Consent

Where applicable, FLEXEN may provide a cookie consent mechanism that allows visitors to indicate their preferences for non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

Your selected preferences may be stored so that the website can recognise your choice during subsequent visits, subject to the configuration of the relevant consent system.

Essential technologies that are necessary for the website to operate may continue to function even where optional cookies are declined.

12. Changes to This Cookie Policy

FLEXEN may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, technologies, services, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date displayed at the beginning of this policy.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically to remain informed about how cookies and similar technologies may be used.
