How to Find Reliable Suppliers in China

A practical guide to researching, evaluating and comparing potential Chinese suppliers for your business.

Finding a supplier is only the first step. Businesses sourcing from China need to evaluate potential suppliers carefully before committing to an order or long-term procurement relationship.

1. Start With a Clear Product Requirement

Before approaching suppliers, define exactly what you need. Include product specifications, materials, dimensions, quantity, packaging requirements and any quality expectations.

2. Identify Potential Suppliers

Research suppliers that appear to manufacture or supply the type of product you require. Do not rely on one supplier alone. Building a shortlist allows you to compare options.

3. Review the Supplier's Product Range

Examine whether the supplier's existing product range matches your requirements. A supplier with relevant experience may be better positioned to understand your specifications.

4. Ask Detailed Questions

Ask about product specifications, production capacity, minimum order quantities, lead times, packaging and payment terms.

5. Compare Quotations

Request quotations from multiple potential suppliers where practical. Compare what is actually included in each quotation rather than comparing the headline price alone.

6. Check Product Specifications

Make sure the supplier understands your exact requirements. Small differences in material, dimensions, finish, packaging or performance can significantly affect the final product.

7. Consider Minimum Order Quantities

Minimum order quantities can have a major effect on the economics of a sourcing project. Confirm the supplier's requirements before proceeding.

8. Consider Logistics Early

Supplier selection should not be separated from logistics. Shipping method, cargo volume, destination and delivery requirements can influence the overall cost.

9. Compare the Total Procurement Cost

The supplier's unit price is only one component of the overall cost. Consider product cost, packaging, shipping, clearing and other applicable expenses when evaluating options.

10. Work With a Structured Sourcing Process

Supplier research becomes easier when the process is structured. Start with your requirements, identify potential suppliers, compare options and then move into commercial discussions.
