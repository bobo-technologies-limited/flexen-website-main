Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

FLEXEN ("FLEXEN", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how we may collect, receive, use, store, protect, and otherwise process personal information when you visit our website, submit an enquiry, request a quotation, communicate with us, or use our services.

By using the FLEXEN website, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy and understand how your information may be handled as described below.

Our Privacy Approach

FLEXEN aims to collect only information that is reasonably relevant to operating our website, responding to enquiries, providing services, supporting sourcing activities, and managing our business relationships.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with FLEXEN, we may collect different categories of information.

Information You Provide Directly

This may include information you voluntarily provide when completing forms, requesting a quotation, contacting us, or communicating with our team.

  • Full name.
  • Business or company name.
  • Email address.
  • Telephone or mobile number.
  • Business address or delivery location.
  • Product or sourcing requirements.
  • Quantity and product specifications.
  • Budget or commercial requirements where voluntarily provided.
  • Information contained in messages, enquiries, quotations, or other communications.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically depending on the technologies implemented on the website.

  • IP address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device type.
  • Operating system.
  • Referring website or page.
  • Pages visited.
  • Approximate usage and interaction data.
  • Technical information relating to website performance and security.

3. Information Submitted Through Our Forms

FLEXEN may provide forms through which visitors can request quotations, submit sourcing requirements, contact our team, or make other enquiries.

Information submitted through these forms may be used to understand your requirements, respond to your request, communicate with you, prepare quotations, and determine how FLEXEN may assist you.

You should avoid submitting sensitive personal information through a website form unless it is specifically requested and there is a legitimate reason for providing it.

4. How We May Use Your Information

FLEXEN may use information collected through the website and our business interactions for purposes including:

  • Responding to enquiries and requests.
  • Preparing or discussing quotations.
  • Understanding product and sourcing requirements.
  • Identifying appropriate sourcing opportunities.
  • Communicating with clients and prospective clients.
  • Providing and improving our services.
  • Managing business relationships.
  • Coordinating sourcing, procurement, logistics, shipping, or related services.
  • Maintaining website security.
  • Detecting and preventing misuse or fraudulent activity.
  • Understanding website performance and usage.
  • Meeting applicable legal, regulatory, or administrative requirements.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable, FLEXEN may process personal information on the basis of one or more lawful grounds recognised by applicable privacy and data protection requirements.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include:

  • Your consent.
  • Taking steps at your request before entering into a business relationship or contract.
  • Performing contractual or business obligations.
  • Complying with legal or regulatory obligations.
  • Legitimate business purposes where permitted by applicable law.

Where processing is based on consent, you may have the right to withdraw that consent, subject to applicable legal requirements and any consequences of doing so.

6. Communications

If you provide your contact information, FLEXEN may use it to respond to your enquiry, provide requested information, discuss sourcing requirements, or communicate about an existing business relationship.

Where permitted by applicable law, FLEXEN may also send business-related communications that may be relevant to your relationship with us.

You may request that certain non-essential communications stop, subject to applicable legal and contractual requirements.

7. When We May Share Information

FLEXEN may disclose personal information where reasonably necessary to operate our business, provide requested services, comply with legal requirements, or protect our rights.

Depending on the circumstances, information may be shared with:

  • Service providers supporting our business operations.
  • Technology and website service providers.
  • Logistics, shipping, clearing, or related service providers where necessary to provide requested services.
  • Suppliers or business partners where sharing information is necessary to respond to a sourcing or procurement requirement.
  • Professional advisers where reasonably necessary.
  • Government authorities, regulators, courts, or law enforcement bodies where required or permitted by applicable law.

Important: FLEXEN aims to limit information sharing to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose, service, business relationship, or legal obligation.

8. International Data Transfers

FLEXEN works with international sourcing markets and may interact with suppliers, service providers, logistics partners, or other organisations located outside Kenya.

Where personal information is transferred or accessed across borders, FLEXEN will seek to handle such information in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

The safeguards applicable to a particular transfer may depend on the nature of the information, the recipient, the purpose of the transfer, and applicable legal requirements.

9. Data Retention

FLEXEN may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, maintain business records, provide services, resolve disputes, comply with legal or regulatory obligations, or protect our legitimate business interests.

Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the information and the reason it was collected.

When personal information is no longer required, FLEXEN may securely delete, anonymise, or otherwise dispose of it in accordance with applicable requirements and our internal procedures.

10. Data Security

FLEXEN takes reasonable organisational, technical, and administrative measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, misuse, loss, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Depending on the nature of the information and the relevant system, security measures may include access controls, authentication, secure communications, restricted access, monitoring, backups, and other appropriate safeguards.

Security Limitation

No internet transmission, electronic storage system, or website can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Visitors should therefore exercise appropriate care when submitting information online.

11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

FLEXEN may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences, understand website usage, improve performance, and support security.

For more information about cookies and how they may be managed, please review our Cookie Policy .

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances of the processing, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

These may include rights to:

  • Request information about how your personal information is processed.
  • Request access to personal information held about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where applicable.
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise a concern or complaint regarding the handling of personal information.

These rights are subject to applicable legal requirements and limitations.

13. Privacy Requests

If you wish to make a privacy-related request, you can contact FLEXEN through our Contact page .

To help us process your request, you may need to provide sufficient information to identify yourself and explain the nature of your request.

We may need to verify your identity before providing access to, changing, or deleting personal information.

14. Children's Privacy

The FLEXEN website and services are intended primarily for businesses, organisations, professionals, and other users capable of entering into business relationships.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to our legitimate business activities.

15. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to websites, platforms, or services operated by third parties.

Third-party websites have their own privacy practices, policies, and terms. FLEXEN is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of third-party websites before submitting personal information to them.

16. Business Contact Information

FLEXEN may process contact information relating to representatives of businesses, suppliers, partners, customers, and prospective customers as part of ordinary business activities.

Such information may be used for purposes such as communicating about sourcing requirements, quotations, procurement activities, logistics, partnerships, account management, and related business matters.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

FLEXEN may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, business practices, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page and the "Last Updated" date will be revised.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to remain informed about how we handle personal information.

18. Related Policies

Additional information about the use of the FLEXEN website and our services can be found in the following documents:

  • Terms of Service
  • Cookie Policy
  • Disclaimer

19. Contact FLEXEN

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how FLEXEN handles personal information, please contact us through our Contact page .

If your enquiry relates to sourcing or procurement, you can also submit your requirements through our Get a Quote page.
