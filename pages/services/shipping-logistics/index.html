FLEXEN helps businesses coordinate shipping and logistics requirements after supplier and procurement decisions have been made.

Does FLEXEN help coordinate shipping?

FLEXEN can support the coordination of shipping and logistics as part of the wider international sourcing and procurement process.

Can shipping be coordinated from China?

Yes. China is one of the sourcing markets supported through the FLEXEN procurement and logistics process.

What happens when the goods reach the destination port?

Once goods arrive at the destination port, they can move into the appropriate port clearing and onward delivery process.

Can shipping be part of a complete sourcing request?

Yes. Shipping can be coordinated alongside supplier matching, price negotiation, payment facilitation and port clearing.
