THE SERVICE

Simplifying the Payment Stage of International Procurement

Once a suitable supplier and procurement option have been identified, payment becomes an important part of moving the transaction forward.

FLEXEN provides payment facilitation as part of its broader sourcing and procurement coordination process, helping businesses navigate the payment stage after procurement terms have been reviewed and approved.

WHY IT MATTERS

Keep Procurement Moving After Supplier Approval

International sourcing involves several connected stages. Payment is one of the key steps between supplier approval and logistics.

One Coordinated Process

Keep payment connected to supplier engagement, procurement and logistics rather than treating each stage separately.

Reduced Coordination Burden

Work through one procurement coordination process instead of managing every stage independently.

Clear Procurement Progression

Connected Logistics Support

BEFORE YOU START

Understanding Payment Facilitation

Is payment facilitation separate from sourcing?

Payment facilitation is designed to form part of the broader sourcing and procurement process.

When does payment coordination happen?

It generally follows supplier identification, commercial review and approval of the procurement option.

Can payment support work with logistics?

Yes. Payment can form one stage of a wider procurement journey that continues into shipping and port-clearing coordination.

How do I begin?

Submit your sourcing requirement and provide the relevant procurement details so FLEXEN can understand what you need.
