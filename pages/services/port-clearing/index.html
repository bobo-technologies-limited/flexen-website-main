THE SERVICE

Coordinating the Journey from Port to Destination

Importing goods does not end when a shipment arrives at the port. Clearing and onward movement are important stages in getting goods from an international supplier to the business that ordered them.

FLEXEN helps coordinate the port-clearing stage as part of its wider sourcing, shipping and procurement support, helping businesses move their imported goods toward their final destination.

BEFORE YOU START

Understanding Port Clearing

What happens when goods arrive at the port?

Imported goods enter the clearing stage before they can proceed toward their intended local destination.

Does port clearing connect with shipping?

Yes. Clearing follows the international shipping stage and forms part of the wider import logistics process.

Can FLEXEN coordinate the wider process?

Port clearing can be connected with FLEXEN's sourcing, payment and shipping coordination services.

How do I get started?

Submit your sourcing requirement and provide the relevant product, supplier, shipment and delivery information available to you.
